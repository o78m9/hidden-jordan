Purpose of the Website

Hidden Jordan is an academic student website that helps university students think about simple and low-cost trip ideas inside Jordan. The website is not a booking service, not a travel company, and not an official source of tourism information.

The content is written as a planning guide. It uses general route categories, sample student budgets, and preparation ideas without giving official prices, schedules, or entry information.

How to Use This Website

Start with the destination ideas, compare the budget levels, then use the planner page as a worksheet before discussing the trip with your friends or classmates.

Plan a Student Trip in Three Steps

  1. Choose the type of experience you want.
  2. Compare the budget level and the preparation needed.
  3. Write your plan in the Student Escape Planner worksheet.

Website Sections

  • Destinations gives four academic route ideas.
  • Planner gives a simple form and manual worksheet.
  • About explains the mission and sample stories.
  • Contact gives support information and a contact form.

Sample Student Budget Levels

Planning example only: budget levels are not official prices.
Budget Level Meaning Useful For
Very Low Budget Choose free or nearly free activities and bring food from home. Walking days and nearby places.
Low Budget Share transportation and keep the plan short. Small group day trips.
Medium Budget Add more comfort while still planning carefully. Longer study break trips.
