Northern Heritage Day

A calm day plan for students who enjoy older streets, heritage places, and group discussion. It is useful for classmates who want a learning-focused escape.

Experience type: Heritage and culture.

Budget level: Low Budget.

Suggested duration: One day.

Bring With You

  • Water bottle.
  • Notebook or phone notes.
  • Comfortable shoes.
  • Simple snacks.

Cost-saving tip: Travel with classmates and agree on a simple food plan before leaving.

Green Nature Escape

A simple outdoor route idea for students who want fresh air after exams or long study weeks. The plan focuses on preparation, safety, and keeping the day affordable.

Experience type: Nature and relaxation.

Budget level: Very Low Budget.

Suggested duration: Half day or one day.

Bring With You

  • Water.
  • Light jacket when needed.
  • Small first aid items.
  • Food prepared at home.

Cost-saving tip: Choose a nearby place and avoid buying many items during the trip.

Old Town Walking Day

A walking-based plan for students who prefer short trips, easy movement, and simple observation of local streets and markets. It works well for small student groups.

Experience type: Walking and local discovery.

Budget level: Very Low Budget.

Suggested duration: Half day.

Bring With You

  • Comfortable shoes.
  • Small amount of cash for personal needs.
  • Charged phone.
  • Reusable water bottle.

Cost-saving tip: Keep the route walkable and focus on free observation activities.

Desert Study Break

A planning example for students who want a quiet break from campus life. It focuses on group preparation, simple supplies, and respectful behavior in natural places.

Experience type: Open landscape and study break.

Budget level: Medium Budget.

Suggested duration: One long day.

Bring With You

  • Extra water.
  • Sun protection.
  • Shared snacks.
  • Clear return plan.

Cost-saving tip: Share transportation fairly and avoid adding unnecessary stops.

Resource Tips

  • Plan early and agree on the route before leaving.
  • Share transportation when it is suitable and safe.
  • Bring water and food to reduce extra spending.
  • Respect places, people, and local rules.
  • Keep a clear return plan for the whole group.
